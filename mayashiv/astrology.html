01 What a consultation is

The Maya half of the idea.

This site is built on two ideas — Maya, the pattern the world presents to us, and Shiv, the awareness that learns to see it clearly. Astrology belongs to the first: a traditional Indian reading of the shape and timing of a life, worked out from the chart. Counselling belongs to the second.

A consultation is therefore a conversation, not a pronouncement. The chart is read properly and explained in plain language, questions are taken, and the two of us look at what it suggests about the matter you brought in. What you do with that is yours.

  • The readingTraditional Indian astrology (jyotish) from the birth chart — the position of the planets at your birth and how they move now.
  • The purposePerspective on a situation you are inside of and cannot see from the outside — especially one that has a decision or a date attached to it.
  • The mannerPlain language, no jargon left unexplained, and no answers invented to fill a silence.
  • The limitsStated openly further down this page. They matter as much as the reading does.

02 What people bring

Questions with a date attached.

Most consultations are about direction or timing. A few are simply about understanding a stretch of life that has been difficult for reasons that are hard to name.

  • Career & studyA choice between paths, a course or stream, a change of field, or whether this is the year to move — read alongside the academic and counselling conversation rather than instead of it.
  • TimingWhen to start, apply, sign, move or wait. Timing questions are the ones astrology is most often consulted for, and the ones where honest uncertainty matters most.
  • Periods that are difficultUnderstanding why a phase has felt heavy, what it is typically about, and how long the pattern is said to run.
  • Relationships & familyUnderstanding a bond, or a family question that keeps returning. Consultations involving another person's chart are only taken with their knowledge.
  • The year aheadA general reading of the period to come, for those who want context before the decisions arrive rather than after.

03 How it works

1 · Send your details

Date, time and place of birth, together with the question or situation you want to discuss. Accurate birth time matters — more on that below.

2 · Appointment fixed

A time is agreed, accommodating your timezone, and a Zoom, Teams or Meet link is sent to you in advance.

3 · Consultation

A 45–60 minute session, with the chart explained and your questions taken. A written note of the main points can follow, on request.

  • PreparationThe chart is prepared before the appointment, so the session itself is spent on your questions rather than on spelling out the basics.
  • Birth timeIf the exact time is unknown or approximate, say so. The reading can still be done, but its precision changes — and you will be told where it does.
  • LanguagesEnglish · Hindi · Bengali · Tamil · Assamese
  • FeesShared before the appointment is confirmed. Nothing is payable until a time is agreed.

04 What this is not

  • Not a prediction of certaintyAstrology reads tendencies and timings. It cannot tell you what will happen, and anyone who claims otherwise is selling something.
  • Not fear-basedNo session is built around a threat. Nothing here is designed to frighten you into buying a remedy, a ritual or a follow-up.
  • No compulsory remediesIf a traditional remedy is mentioned, it is mentioned as tradition and as optional. You will never be told your problem cannot be solved without spending more.
  • Not medical, legal or financial adviceFor health, legal or investment decisions, see a qualified professional. A consultation is not a substitute and will not pretend to be.
  • Not a crisis serviceIf you are in distress or in danger, please reach a doctor, a helpline or someone you trust first. Sessions are scheduled appointments, not emergency support.
  • Not about other people's privacyQuestions asked about a third person without their knowledge are declined — their chart is not mine to read for you.
  • Your decision remains yoursGuidance is offered, and then you decide. Nothing discussed in a session obliges you to act on it.
A chart shows the weather you were born into. It does not decide how you walk through it — and it is not a substitute for a decision you are avoiding making.
