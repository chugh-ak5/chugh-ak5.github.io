03 What the work covers

Curriculum governance

Activity-based planning and quality assurance against CBSE, ICSE and IB frameworks — scheme-of-work discipline, assessment design and audit-ready documentation.

Faculty mentorship

Lesson observations, structured feedback loops and workshops that move a teacher from delivering a syllabus to designing learning.

Student welfare

Psychological guidance, behavioural counselling and career roadmapping — with parents brought into the room rather than kept outside it.

Intercultural education

Exchange programmes, selection interviews, orientation camps and global-citizenship work with AFS across schools in Chennai and South India.

Institutional administration

Centre operations, faculty scheduling, ISO-standard quality compliance, policy adherence, budgeting and audit readiness.

Communication & publications

Parent and stakeholder communication, PTA management, in-house academic newsletters, and media-literacy programmes with The Hindu's NIE initiative.
