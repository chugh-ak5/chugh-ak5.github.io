03 Arts & culture

Where children
find their voice

Curriculum gets a school its results. The arts, the debates and the service projects are what a child carries out of the gate. I have spent as much time on the stage, in the auditorium and on the excursion bus as I have in the staff room — and I would defend that balance to any management.

01 Theatre & the spoken word

English theatrical direction

Annual Day productions directed end to end — casting, rehearsal discipline, diction, staging — at Bhavan's Rajaji Vidyashram and in earlier schools. A school play is the one place a shy child is given a reason to speak loudly and a reason to be listened to.

Elocution and debate coaching across inter-school championships: teaching a student to build an argument, hold it under pressure, and concede a point without collapsing.

  • DirectionAnnual Day theatrical productions — casting, rehearsal scheduling, staging and performance standards.
  • ElocutionMentoring students for inter-school elocution, quiz and debate championships.
  • Public speakingVoice, pacing and presence — coached as teachable skills rather than natural gifts.
  • Model UNInter-school MUN and quiz contests, where a student must argue a position that isn't their own.

02 Music

Classical music, formally trained

An entire bachelor's degree in Indian classical music — not a hobby, a discipline. It shapes how I hear a room: pitch, timing, who is waiting to come in, who has gone quiet. Teachers who can hear rhythm notice a struggling child early.

Concerts, inter-house competitions and school choirs were run with the same standards as the academic timetable.

  • 1984B.A. Indian Classical Music — Chandigarh University
  • PracticeConcerts, inter-house music competitions and school ensemble work.
  • ApplicationRhythm and listening used as classroom tools — attention trained through sound as much as through text.

03 Service & community

Attention to other people's realities.

The Shiv half of the idea has to be practised somewhere concrete. In my schools it was practised in service clubs, literacy drives and exchange programmes.

  • Social service clubSupervised the School Social Service Club at Bhavan's Rajaji Vidyashram, organising community engagement and philanthropic drives for partner institutions such as The Banyan, which works with destitute women.
  • Community literacySpearheaded community literacy programmes — the 3 R's — alongside school and Parent–Teacher Association activity in Kolkata.
  • Intercultural exchangeWith AFS, coordinated intercultural learning and exchange initiatives for middle and secondary students across Chennai and South India, including orientation camps and adaptation mentoring.
  • ExcursionsPlanned and led educational excursions — the part of the syllabus that teaches students how to behave in an unfamiliar place.
A child who has stood on a stage, argued a case and worked a day for someone with nothing has already met the world. That is not extra-curricular. That is the curriculum working.
