03 Current work

Building the plumbing I used to wish existed.

04 Principle

Position sizing is a risk decision, not a conviction decision. The strategy can be wrong; the risk process cannot be.

Six years of trading under drawdown constraints teaches the same lesson in different currencies: the traders who survive are the ones whose worst day was designed in advance.
