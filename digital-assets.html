05 Digital assets

The venue that
never closes.

Crypto taught me the part of markets that traditional hours politely hide: liquidity is fragile, funding is a price, and settlement risk is something you design around rather than assume away. I've worked the desk side of it — rotating shifts, 50+ pairs, real order books — and I now model the on-chain side in code.

01 Exchange desk

Liquidity is a service, not a property.

On the trading desk at LATOKEN I ran 24/7 operations across rotational shifts: order-book depth surveillance, liquidity routing, and quote integrity across connected exchange endpoints. Fifty-plus digital asset pairs, no daylight saving, no closing bell.

The job is mostly vigilance. A markup spread that drifts, a liquidity provider quoting wide, a feed that lags by 800ms — each one is an arbitrage gap for someone and a credibility problem for you. Catching it first is the entire product.

  • Book integrityDepth surveillance across pairs; liquidity-provider quote monitoring to keep spreads competitive and honest.
  • Feed disciplineReal-time pricing feeds and markup spreads watched for staleness, drift and cross-venue divergence.
  • ReconciliationAutomated trade-matching verification, deposit/withdrawal flow reconciliation and cross-feed break resolution across exchange nodes.
  • ContinuityRotational shift handovers with written state — because "the book looked fine at 06:00" is not a handover.

02 Treasury & rails

Moving value between two worlds.

Crypto treasuries live in a permanent exchange-rate problem: value sits in stablecoins, obligations sit in fiat, and the bridge between them is where operational risk actually lives.

Store

Stablecoin inventory

Working balances across stablecoin venues, with issuer, chain and redemption profile treated as distinct risks rather than as "cash".

Route

Multi-currency movement

Routing across 12+ fiat currencies with SWIFT and SEPA workflows — cut-off times, correspondent behaviour and fees are part of the pricing, not an afterthought.

Control

Reconciliation first

Daily position and payment reconciliation, exception-first, so a break surfaces as a task rather than as a month-end surprise.

03 On-chain risk

A lending market you can read in real time.

What interests me about DeFi lending is that the risk is legible. Reserve utilisation, interest-rate curves, collateral factors and obligation health factors are all on-chain state you can query directly — no waiting for a disclosure, no guessing at a bank's book. Solana's Kamino Lending is the clearest example I've worked with.

So I built the instrument I wanted: Kamino Sentinel tracks those reserves and obligations continuously, computes liquidation prices and stress scenarios, and dispatches an alert while there's still time to act. The lesson transfers directly to a traditional risk desk — if a position's failure mode can be computed, it should be computed continuously, not quarterly.

  • Health factorCollateral value against liquidation threshold — the number that determines whether a position survives a wick.
  • Utilisation & ratesReserve utilisation drives the borrow curve; a kinked curve means liquidity can evaporate exactly when demand peaks.
  • Liquidation mechanicsLiquidators are paid to be fast. Solana's block times and Jito-style bundle routing mean the race is measured in milliseconds, not minutes.
  • Stress testingScenario matrices over collateral and borrow shocks, with an unwind plan costed in advance — including flash-unwind paths that need no fresh capital.

04 Vocabulary

TermDesk translation
Funding rateThe price of leverage, paid between perpetual longs and shorts. A funding print is a positioning survey.
BasisPerpetual or futures versus spot — the cleanest read on who is paying for exposure and in which direction.
DepthHow much size moves the price. Reported volume without depth tells you almost nothing about execution quality.
Health factorDistance to liquidation. On-chain analogue of a margin call, visible continuously instead of once a day.
MEVValue extracted from ordering transactions. Ignoring it is a choice; on Solana, bundle routing is how you compete for inclusion.
Settlement riskWhether the counterparty delivers. Stablecoins reduce transfer latency, not issuer or de-peg risk.

05 Position

Crypto is genuinely better at settlement, availability and programmability. It is not better at credit assessment, and any structure that pretends otherwise is repackaging the same risk with fewer disclosures.

That's the posture I bring to it: use the rails where they're structurally superior, refuse the parts that are leverage wearing a new vocabulary, and keep the risk maths in code where it can be tested.
