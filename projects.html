01 Flagship

Kamino Sentinel

High-frequency monitoring, risk telemetry and a liquidation-defense engine for Kamino Lending on Solana — built for the borrower who would rather see the health factor move than discover it after the liquidation.

Observe

Reserve & obligation telemetry

Continuous tracking of on-chain reserve utilisation, interest-rate curves and user obligation health factors, parsed straight from the lending program's accounts.

Model

Stress shocks & unwind maths

Liquidation-price calculation and multi-scenario stress matrices, plus atomic flash-unwind planning so a de-leverage path exists before it's needed.

Act

Alerts & execution routing

Alert dispatch with cooldown suppression to Telegram and webhooks, and Jito MEV bundle routing for unwind transactions that need to land atomically.

02 Desk tooling

Built for the desk, not the résumé.

Two of the systems I use most are internal: the strategy research rig and the ops stack that watches my infrastructure. They aren't public, so here is what they do rather than a link that goes nowhere.

strategy-research-rig / private

Event-driven backtesting framework for index futures. It exists to kill bad ideas cheaply: session-aware fills, commission and slippage modelled per contract, walk-forward splits, and an out-of-sample window that gets touched exactly once. A strategy survives the rig before it ever sees a live account.

Python

Event-drivenCost realismWalk-forwardFutures sessions

ops-stack / private

The alerting and hygiene layer for everything I run: scheduled monitors for market data and price targets, log and disk checks on headless cloud instances, backup verification, and a single dispatch path to Telegram so an alert can't drown in email. Built to run unattended on free-tier infrastructure.

04 How I work

  • Test the maths, not the vibes Liquidation, unwind and P&L arithmetic gets unit tests before it gets a live RPC endpoint.
  • Fail loudly, then quietly RPC failover, retries with backoff, and alert throttling so a flapping feed doesn't train anyone to ignore the channel.
  • Ship it runnable Dockerfile, compose, environment template and CI in the repo — a project nobody else can start in five minutes isn't finished.
  • Read the source, always Protocol behaviour comes from the program accounts and the docs, not from a blog post about them.

Have a desk problem worth automating?

Reconciliation, risk telemetry, alerting, backtest infrastructure — that's the work I enjoy most.
